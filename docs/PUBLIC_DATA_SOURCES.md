Audit date: 2026-10-11. The source counts below distinguish metadata, images, masks, videos, and model assets. An artwork image is not a gallery scene, and a contact label is not a painting-protection label.

## Video inventory

No public video files are present locally. ContactPose’s public sample route was not downloaded because its linked Drive page requested sign-in and an anonymous size preflight could not be confirmed. HOI4D’s official OneDrive/Baidu release did not expose a bounded clip size suitable for a safe first-stage download. No arbitrary videos, scraped sites, or YouTube downloads were used.

## Label and mask boundaries

- Open Images boxes were not converted to masks. Its original mask PNGs and nearest-neighbor image-aligned PNGs remain separate.
- There are no real painting masks, hand masks, gallery scenes, or painting-contact labels in the local real data.
- Synthetic contact scenes are kept under `dataset/synthetic/art_contact/`, have exact binary masks and deterministic source-grouped splits, and are marked `SYNTHETIC`.
- See [DATASET_LICENSES.md](DATASET_LICENSES.md) and the machine-readable [dataset report](DATASET_REPORT.md) for acquisition hashes and the current validation results.
